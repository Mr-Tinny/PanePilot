PanePilot 1.0.0.37 adds volume controls to both fullscreen views. In a player's own fullscreen mode, drag the slider beside the pane title. In PanePilot's F11 mode, move the pointer near the top to adjust the selected stream. Both sliders show the current percentage and work with the layout locked.

Other changes since 1.0.0.27 include:

- Choose **View → Lock layout** to keep panes in place while watching. PanePilot remembers the lock when you reopen it.
- Drag one pane title onto another to swap two streams. Use **View → Swap panes** in fullscreen, and **Edit → Undo swap panes** to reverse a swap.
- Paste several stream links at once, name each pane, and choose your main stream.
- Press **Ctrl+Shift+1–9** to listen to one pane, **Ctrl+Shift+T** to reopen a closed pane, or **F1** to see the shortcuts.

**Windows x64 portable download:** Extract the entire ZIP and run `PanePilot.exe` from the extracted folder. To update an existing copy, close PanePilot and replace the files in its existing folder.
